ALSO Gemma shared some art she is making of Quana and I recently read the bright queen comics (really good btw I can point you to a free source if u want) so have a lot of Quana and Leylas on the mind. She sent this image which is a crazy departure from lore Quana BUT it actually got my mind going wild. we were discussing logos and integrating the Luxon etc when I realised she looks nothing like the heavy armoured warrior in canon... a brainwave hit me when Gemma said she was thinking of Quana as a lone-wolf-ronin out there doing good deeds, hoping to stumble across Leylas somewhere.

At this point I was like OMFG that's Leylas' sword, only put the logo on that. this flowing dress style is representative of her moving on after losing Leylas forever, but she kept her sword. Everything else is like her coming out of the cocoon that was the Kryn Dynasty

basically this art ended up being a prompt that I SORELY needed to get in Quanas headspace as I was struggling to think of her in this story

I'm thinking she has a lil cameo in Bazzoxan, she stopped over there after a job or something

But this would be an encounter after betrayers rise and before they leave for marquet
