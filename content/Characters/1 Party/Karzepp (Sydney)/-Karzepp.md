#### Current Situation 
> **Karzepp Character Reflections**
Mission goals: To bring healing to her forest, the Lotusden Greenwood. The good of the many and the whole is more important that Karzepp personally. The safety and flourishing of the Lotusden is more important that Krazepp’s personal significance or healing. She is a small creature in a big forest. She seeks her place in the world. Her corner in which to hide.
**Big question: Am I the corruption or am I the cure?** 
Bonds: The forest and its array of denizens and complex ecosystem is her home. 
She is not afraid in the shadows. No matter how fierce, everyone has their place.  
As a druid of this forest, it is the source of her power. The druidic tradition from which she’s been corrupted would be circle of the land (I think).
Survival strategies: As an individual, a smile or a retreat have always kept her safe. In the community, among the small in the forest, you either thrive in numbers together as a swarm or you find a big beast to whom to hitch your fortune. 
Ability themes: corruption (ruidium, poison, necrotic), forest (plants and water), adding moon because of Moonweaver.
**Adventure Moments and Relationships**
**Belonging**
Leaving Greenwood – physical disconnection but deep longing to return
Bugsy and the Myriad goblins – connection and naive kind of trust. In Brazoxxan these are the little ones “down in the undergrowth” they are Karzepp’s people.
Angela Clasp – Karzepp feels affinity for one who trucks in potions, poisons, and illicit goods. Karzepp likes the potential of a “partner in crime” who enjoys mischief, dark humour, and feels ok if someone gets hurt in the process.
**Party**
Karzepp knows that she is a small halfling in a large Greenwood. She needs a swarm or a big power to hide among or behind. Oma and Kurri feel almost one and the same in this regard. They are angry beasts that you befriend so that when someone attacks, the angry beast defends you. 
Karzepp still feels that Natsumi makes much more sense as a leader to free Alyxion. Natsumi is full of wonder and magic. Natsumi is someone important. Natsumi rules an empire (Karzepp’s twisting of Natsumi’s story). Natsumi is not akin to a beast but rather one of the powerful magical spirits of the woods who would twist reality around themselves and keep the goblins safe. 
Both Oma and Natsumi are powerful ally material, but neither is in a “friend” category to Karzepp. On the other hand, Coco and Ular are friendshaped. Karzepp places herself with the companions and the familiars rather than the protagonists.
**Family**
**Tillie/Oma** – Surface level acceptance and treating Oma in a way the mirrors Karzepp’s experience of other people with their families. Karzepp is playing a part rather than feeling connected. Infact, Karzepp is sensitive to the disconnect between the part being played and the fact that Oma never showed up. 
**Sunny reveal as Denderon/Paman** – confusion. Maybe Oma isn’t committed. Blood family isn’t important to her? But Oma seems protective of Karzepp and maybe(?) there is a fear of the past repeating itself and Paman taking away the young druid under Oma’s watch. So is Oma actually committed to Karzepp or to Oma’s own past trauma and issues? Karzepp feels an affinity to Paman and more of a family resemblance to him than to Oma. 
**Father and Mother** – running out of jam makes Karzepp miss Jammi fiercely. The coincidence of this with Denderon’s reveal does make Karzepp have at least a moment of wondering who else she doesn’t know and where they might be. For the first time in a long time, Karzepp considers her mother.
**Divine**
**Melora** – Karzepp’s druidic circle has origins with Melora. Jammi has gifted Karzepp an openness to divine things and larger powers, but Karzepp is not tied exclusively to Melora. Melora is comfortable but not dominating for Karzepp. The forest rather than the goddess is what matters, hence druid not cleric.
**Sehanine/Moonweaver** – vision in Jigow. The call from Alyxion fills Karzepp with compassion for someone who is stuck. And the ruidium connection makes Karzepp think this might be linked to healing the forest. Karzepp is more than ready to help. Karzepp welcomes the moon and connection with the Moonweaver. Karzepp doesn’t identify as a powerful player. 
	The commandments of the Moonweaver are:
	*Let the shadows protect you from the burning light of fanaticism and the absolute darkness of despair.*
	*Walk unbridled and untethered, forging new memories and experiences.*
	*Seize your own destiny and pursue your own passions.*
Karzepp easily embraces the first two of these. But she is fearful and reluctant to follow the third. This is part of the existential discomfort of not being sure of her place and identity. She wants healing for her forest, that is her destiny and passion but the violence of “seizing” it is not something in Karzepp’s nature at the moment.
**Prayer in Brazoxxan** – Wild Mother, Changebringer, Moonweaver. Comfort, affirmation. Further strengthens the desire to free Alyxion and end his suffering, with the foreshadowed connections to healing the forest also. Karzepp will carry the amulet but still thinks the power for the task will come from Natsumi’s brains and Tillie’s strength. Karzepp brings the corruption that is in need of cure. That is her part.

**Ruidium**
Karzepp feels that what she brings to her mission is the red corrupting rock and from the lore they’ve been told and Tillie’s experience, that could be another calamity and horror akin to the barbed fields.
- Uncontrollable rage and waking with blood on your hands
- Red moon dream and demon slaughter of wastewalker tribe
- Corrupt ground and a need to bring down a creature
- Connection with undead/necrotic energy in the cave of the Bright Queen
- Harvesting ruidium but it continues to crystallise and grow, it aches. It’s heavy. Scraping it away doesn’t cure the underlying issue and Karzepp knows that.

##### Idea for Wildshape variant
Inspirations from: Defile ground (druid circle feature), blighted sapling (plant summoned from defiled ground level 6), guardian of nature Great Tree (level 4 spell)
Defile Ground
	Once per short rest, you can use a bonus action to corrupt a patch of land or an area of water in a 10-ft. radius centered on a point within 60 ft. of you. This corruption lasts for 1 minute. The corrupted area is difficult terrain for creatures that are hostile to you. Additionally, when a creature in the area takes damage from an attack or spell for the first time on a turn, it takes an extra 1d4 necrotic damage. You can move this patch of corruption up to 30 ft. as a bonus action.

Great Tree – level 4 spell, learned with 7th level in druid
- 1 bonus action to cast, range Self, Verbal components, concentration, 1 minute. Nature spirit powers transformation. Your skin appears barky, leaves sprout from your hair.
	- 10 temp HP
	- Advantage on Constitution saves
	- Dex and Wis attack rolls with advantage
	- Ground within 15 feet is difficult terrain for enemies

Call of the Shadow seeds Blighted Sapling 
- 1 reaction when a creature takes damage on Defiled ground. Sapling can immediately make an attack. 
	- HP = 2x druid level
	- Vulnerable to fire, because plant
	- Resistant to necrotic and poison
	- Immune to blinded, deafened, poisoned
	- Blindsight to 60ft and blind beyond that.
	- Melee Claw attacks use spell attack modifer, 2d4+proficiency piercing bonus.




#### Ruidium Corrupted Shape: 
- To activate: bonus action, one use of wild shape, and 1g ruidium.
- Learn at minimum level 7 after producing a blighted sapling at level 6.
- Transformation lasts 1 minute and does not require concentration. Karzepp can maintain concentration on a previously cast spell.
- Powered by ruidium corrupted forest spirit.
- Transforms into a twisted plant creature like the blighted sapling rather than an animal (because forest, great tree and sapling). Veins of ruidium rock/flesh weave through the form with other patches of dark necrosis.
- Creates defiled ground effect centred on Karzepp in this form. This defiled ground is 10ft in radius and is difficult terrain for hostile creatures.
- Additionally, when a creature in the defiled ground area takes damage from an attack or spell for the first time on a turn, it takes an extra 1d4 necrotic damage.
- Karzepp can use her reaction to summon a blighted sapling in this defiled ground much as she would upon regular defiled ground.
- Karzepp gains temporary HP = 2 x druid level
- Vulnerable to fire, because plant
- Resistant to necrotic and poison
- Immune to the poisoned condition, immune to frightened.
- Walking and Swim speed 30ft
- AC = 10 + proficiency bonus + 2 from blighted circle feature
- 120ft dark vision + 60ft blind sight
- Karzepp has advantage on attack rolls while in this form and imposes disadvantage on saves against her spells.
- Karzepp can cast cantrips in this form but not levelled spells.
- Melee Weapon Attack: using a branchlike limb Karzepp can attack using her spell attack modifier to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. On a hit it takes the same damage as though from chill touch.
