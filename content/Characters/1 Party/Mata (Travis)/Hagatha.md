Well, first of all, [u/PupOgrehas](https://www.reddit.com/user/PupOgrehas/) the right idea - hags are known for their "weird magic" and for making use of nontrivial, eccentric, and unique flavors of magic. You can check out _Volo's Guide to Monsters_ for inspiration and some pretty neat and extensive lore on hags. Also, try to incorporate that said lore.

Night hags, unlike other types of hags who are fey, are fiendish creatures. They enjoy nothing more than corrupting souls of mortal, which they then collect. Night hags are closely associated with themes of night (obviously), darkness and shadow, dreams and nightmares, corruption and manipulation, death and undeath, lower planes, ethereal plane and etherealness, and things like that.

So, that said, here's some ideas from me for you to probably incorporate or draw some inspiration from:

- In the very middle of the middle room - a huge black millstone covered in glowing runes, slowly turning by itself, scraping and creaking. It's grinding souls into fine soul dust. The shiny brilliant powder is flowing down a little gutter into a jar made out of a humanoid skull.
- Up near the ceiling there are hanging chains, and from those chains - cages of different sizes. Inside those cages are not living creatures but their shadows. They moan quietly and beg for help. Do no trust their pleas.
- Room full of mirrors - on the walls, on every shelf, on every table and desk, and drawer. Mirrors are all made of silver, long blackened from age. If any mirror is approached, instead of a reflection you see some random person, saying mean, harsh, rude things, their face contorted in a grimace of hatred and wrath. The mirrors collect evil thoughts, ideas, and notions of those affected by the hag's corruption.
- Room with a giant hourglass mounted in it. There's no floor underneath it, just the ashy grey ground. In fact, the hourglass is "grows" out of the floor, and the grey ash of the ground that's seen through the glass is its "sand". Ashes are slowly rising up in a narrow thread, up into the top of the hourglass, slowly filling it.
- Big tree, similar to the ones outside, is growing throw the broken floor in this room. It's black and old, and completely leafless. But upon inspection it's clear that this is no tree. This is, in fact, a treant, who is being slowly drained of its powers. Tiny servants of the hags are running around, keeping it alive, so it can still provide hags with whatever resources they are harvesting from it. Wood for wands? Sap for potions? Tears for curses? Who knows.
- There are a lot of magical items that fit "witch-y" theme of hags as is, they just need a little flavor tweaking. For example:

    - Cloak of the Bat that looks like a heavy leather coat. When you look at it closely - you see it's sewn out of hundreds of bat wings
    - Cloak of Arachnida that is actually woven with giant spider's web
    - Big, shiny cauldron, the only clean and shine thing in the room, hanging on the wall, that is actually a Mirror of Life Trapping
    - Robe of Useful Items, but all the items are slightly twisted. A horse comes out as skeletal or ghostly. Instead of a door it creates set of spiky bars/grate. Instead of a window - toothy maw opens in the wall, etc.
    - Tricky items like Bad Luck Stones, Bags of Devouring, etc.
    - Rope of Entanglement woven out of human hair
    - Staves of the Adder and of the Python can work as is, snakes are very "witch-y" and "hag-y"

- Also, think about the visual aesthetic that you want to go with. For example, some ideas:
    - Are they paganistic witches? Make everything inside smell of herbs. The floor is covered in a thick layer of fallen crunchy leaves. Hell, maybe walls are covered in pulsing, ever so slightly moving vines and branches between which imps and pixies are playing catch. There would be very little gold, silver, and iron - but a lot of copper and bronze. In the main room - three wicker figures representing the hags, made out of hay or twigs, tied by strings made out of sinew and red thread.
    - Are they more like Victorian Gothic horror witches? Creaking wooden floors with thick dusty carpets. Empty frames on the walls. Candleless chandeliers slowly swaying under the ceilings. The soundscape here is faint distant whispers, rustling of paper, clanking of chains. In the main room - massive old, dusty portrait on the wall, depicting a young woman, a middle-aged woman, and an old woman in dark, strict Victorian dresses and with veils.
    - Are they all about their witchcraft? Add the sounds of bubbling boiling liquids. Air smells strong - acrid, sweet, bitter, etc. Clanking of bottles and jars can be heard in a distance. Weird "frozen" creatures everywhere - perhaps, their failed experiments? Like, a huge jar with an embalmed hybrid of a gnome and some beast. Big stuffed bear with a human head and bat-like wings. Unfinished voodoo dolls scattered on the floor. In the main room - three tall idols of differing height, crudely carved out of wood, representing the hags.
