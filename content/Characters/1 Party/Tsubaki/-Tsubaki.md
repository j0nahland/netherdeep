- so I'm thinking of playing my oc Tsubaki, she is a living suit of samurai armour filled with pink camellia flowers on the inside who wields a big glaive.

The story is that Tsubaki as an awakened suit of armour has been serving Natsumi's kingdom for generations. Decades ago in Natsumi's old life, she was sent on a mission across the sea but something happened to the ship on her way back and she was marooned on the bottom of the ocean

seeing as she has a duty to the royal family to fulfil and she doesn't need to breathe she just decided to walk along the ocean floor until reaching land. This took way longer than anticipated since time passes differently for constructs and she was moving slower than usual being that far away from the sun, so instead of a journey of a few months, she re-emerged from the sea decades later, after Natsumi was lost and a false ruler had taken over her kingdom

Not knowing any of this, she merrily made her way back to the kingdom and saw that many things were different. being Magic and sworn to serve the real rulers of the kingdom (Natsumi and Quana), she knew that they must still be alive out there somewhere. she tried to prove the false ruler was false but got her butt kicked and was chased out of the kingdom

not knowing what to do next, she resolved to find Natsumi, the queen, and try to get some answers/put her back on the throne, but ofc, when she does find Natsumi she is a different person and has no memories of the past

character themes/conflicts I'm thinking of:

-duty vs free will. Now that Natsumi doesn't even know she is the queen, does Tsubaki still have a role to fill? What does she want to do with her life? 

-truth vs blissful ignorance. Natsumi seems so much happier now that she's not the queen. Should Tsubaki tell her the truth about who she is and ruin that? if she doesn't then what about the kingdom and her duty?

-living in the past. A lot of things have changed around her, often to a point where some things are unrecognizable. How can she continue living in this world?
